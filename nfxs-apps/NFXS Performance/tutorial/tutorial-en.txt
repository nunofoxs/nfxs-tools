NFXS | PERFORMANCE
===================

HOW TO OPEN
-----------
Open "NFXS Hub" and click the "NFXS Performance" tile.

The first time, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click
"More info" and then "Run anyway".


STEP BY STEP
------------
1. Choose the game: FiveM or RedM.
2. The tool immediately shows: Windows, CPU, RAM, graphics card, and
   free disk space on the game's drive - green when it meets the
   official requirement, red when it's below it.
3. At the bottom, a summary: "Meets requirements" or "Below
   requirements".
4. Want to check again? Click "Check again".
