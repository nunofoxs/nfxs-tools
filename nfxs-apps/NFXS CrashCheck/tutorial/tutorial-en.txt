NFXS | CRASHCHECK
=================

HOW TO OPEN
-----------
Open "NFXS Hub" and click the "NFXS CrashCheck" tile.

The first time, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click
"More info" and then "Run anyway".


STEP BY STEP
------------
1. The tool immediately shows the list of crashes found for your FiveM
   and RedM, most recent first.
2. Click one in the list to see the result: type of problem, whether
   it's more likely your PC or the server, when it happened, and the
   evidence.
3. Want technical detail (file, hash, stack trace)? Click "View
   technical details".
4. "Copy report" copies a ready-to-paste text for Discord/a ticket.
   "Export files (.zip)" saves the dump, log, and report together to
   your Desktop.
5. Crashed again? Click "Check again" in the list.
