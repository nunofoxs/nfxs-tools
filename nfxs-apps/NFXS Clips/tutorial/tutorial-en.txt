NFXS | CLIPS
============

HOW TO OPEN
-----------
Open "NFXS Hub" and click the "NFXS Clips" tile.

The first time, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click
"More info" and then "Run anyway".

This tool does NOT record anything - Windows itself does the recording
(Win+Alt+G saves the last 30s, Win+Alt+R starts/stops recording,
Win+Alt+PrtScn takes a screenshot). NFXS Clips just organizes what's
already been saved that way.


STEP BY STEP
------------
1. The tool immediately shows your most recent FiveM/RedM screenshots
   and clips, with a thumbnail.
2. Click one to open it with your PC's default app.
3. Can't find the recorder? Click "Open Windows Game Bar (Win+G)".
4. Want to see every file? Click "Open captures folder".
5. Recorded something new? Click "Check again".
