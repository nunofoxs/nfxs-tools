NFXS | CACHE CLEANER
=====================

HOW TO OPEN
-----------
Open "NFXS Hub" and click the "NFXS Cleaner" tile.

The first time, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click
"More info" and then "Run anyway".


STEP BY STEP
------------
1. Choose the game: FiveM or RedM.
2. Choose the mode: "Quick clean (recommended)" or "Full clean".
3. Click "Clean".
4. Done - if you want, check "Open the game after cleaning" before
   step 3 to launch it right after.
