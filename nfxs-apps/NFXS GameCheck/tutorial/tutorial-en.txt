NFXS | GAME CHECK
=================

HOW TO OPEN
-----------
Open "NFXS Hub" and click the "NFXS GameCheck" tile.

The first time, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click
"More info" and then "Run anyway".


STEP BY STEP
------------
1. Choose the game: FiveM or RedM.
2. The tool immediately shows the full diagnostic: installation,
   GTA V/RDR2, graphics mods, compatibility, required components, cache,
   disk space, and whether the game is running.
3. Found "Elevated cache" or similar? Click the link to open
   NFXS Cleaner directly.
4. Fixed something? Click "Check again".
