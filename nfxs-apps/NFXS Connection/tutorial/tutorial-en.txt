NFXS | CONNECTION
=================

HOW TO OPEN
-----------
Open "NFXS Hub" and click the "NFXS Connection" tile.

The first time, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click
"More info" and then "Run anyway".


STEP BY STEP
------------
1. The tool immediately tests your internet and DNS as soon as it
   opens.
2. Pick the game (FiveM or RedM) and, if you want, type the server
   address in IP:port format. The field is optional - without an
   address, you still see whether your internet/DNS are good.
3. The tool shows whether it reached the server, how long it took to
   respond, and its name - just to confirm you tested the right
   server.
4. It also shows a simple ping to the server (latency/packet loss) -
   this is informational only, many servers block ping for security
   even while fully online.
5. In the "Diagnosis" section, the tool combines everything and
   explains in plain language what might be going on.
6. Want to test again? Click "Check again".
